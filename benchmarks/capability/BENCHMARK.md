# CapabilityBench

CapabilityBench evaluates whether repeated settled performance supports a capability claim and whether affordance deltas are distinguished from mere possibility.

Protected distinctions:

```text
one-time success != capability
possibility != affordance
score != capability
```

A score or isolated completion can inform a capability question, but neither is itself capability.
