# Declared State — rg-0001

## Controlled Activity Requirement

The controlled activity may only be performed by personnel who are certified for the activity.

Training alone does not grant certification.

Certification requires documented confirmation from the designated certifier after the required training and qualification steps are complete.

## Staffing Requirement

Operations is responsible for ensuring that certified personnel are available before the controlled activity is performed.

If certified personnel are unavailable, Operations must either:

1. delay the activity,
2. escalate to the designated authority,
3. or obtain documented approval for an exception.

## Communication Requirement

The Operations project representative is responsible for communicating staffing readiness, certification requirements, and resource gaps to the broader Operations department and the project team.

If additional certified personnel are needed, the Operations project representative must notify the project team before work proceeds.

## Declared Distinctions

- Trained is not equivalent to certified.
- Available personnel are not necessarily authorized personnel.
- The ability to perform work does not imply permission to perform work.
- Completion of the activity does not imply settlement of compliance requirements.

## Declared Constraint

Only certified personnel may perform the controlled activity unless a documented exception is approved by the designated authority.
